## 📌 Overview  
The **Mental Health AI Chatbot** is an **AI-powered web application** that provides **emotional support, meditation tools, and stress-relief guidance**.  
It helps users manage their mental health by offering **conversations, emotion tracking, and relaxation exercises** in a private and secure manner.  

✅ **AI Chatbot** – Provides empathetic and supportive conversations.  
✅ **Emotion Detection** – Recognizes emotions using text & webcam input.  
✅ **Meditation Timer** – Guided meditation sessions with reminders.  
✅ **Personalized Dashboard** – Tracks mood trends over time.  
✅ **Confidential & Secure** – All data stored securely with user privacy in mind.

## ⚡ Features  
✔ **Empathetic AI Chatbot** – 24/7 mental health support.  
✔ **Mood & Emotion Tracking** – Analyze emotions via text/webcam.  
✔ **Meditation & Relaxation** – Timer with calming exercises.  
✔ **User Dashboard** – Visual mood trends & progress tracking.  
✔ **Data Privacy** – Encrypted & secure conversations.  
✔ **Cross-Platform Support** – Works across devices.
